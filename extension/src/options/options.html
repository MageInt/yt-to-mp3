yt-to-mp3 capture

The extension captures a video's audio while YouTube plays it, then sends it to your yt-to-mp3 server to be converted. The server never contacts YouTube for this. Original saves the raw capture without any server.

The address you open yt-to-mp3 with.
The CONVERT_TOKEN set on the server. Stored only in this browser profile.

How it works and its limits
  • Audio is copied from what the YouTube player itself downloads: play the video to the end, without skipping. Seeking leaves gaps.
  • Nothing extra is requested from YouTube, so no IP, cookie or "not a bot" issue. It is still against YouTube's terms of service, and YouTube could detect the extension's changes to the page.
  • DRM-protected videos (rented or bought movies…) cannot be captured.
  • The capture lives in the tab's memory until you leave the video or close the tab.
